Lasagna

Description

Lasagna al forno is a classic Italian baked pasta dish composed of layers of thin egg pasta sheets, a slow-simmered meat ragù, creamy besciamella (béchamel) sauce, and grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese. Traditional Northern Italian versions (specifically from Emilia-Romagna) rely heavily on the balance of these components rather than heavy layers of cheese, entirely skipping the ricotta commonly found in Italian-American variations.

Ingredients

  • Pasta: Fresh egg lasagna sheets.
  • The Ragù: A combination of ground beef and ground pork cooked with a aromatic soffritto (onion, carrot, celery), pancetta, white or red wine, tomato passata, and beef stock.
  • The Besciamella: A velvety white sauce made from a butter and flour roux, whole milk, and a pinch of ground nutmeg.
  • The Topping: Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano or Grana Padano cheese.

Steps

  1. Simmer the Ragù:
    • Sauté the aromatics: Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven and gently cook finely chopped onion, carrot, and celery until soft. Add diced pancetta and cook until the fat renders.
    • Brown the meat: Add the ground beef and pork, breaking it apart, and cook until no longer pink.
    • Deglaze: Pour in dry red or white wine and let it reduce by half.
    • Simmer: Stir in tomato paste, passata, a cup of stock, and bay leaves. Cover and simmer on low for 2 to 4 hours, adding more stock as needed to keep it silky and loose.
  2. Whisk the Besciamella:
    • Make the roux: Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat, then whisk in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes until pale blond.
    • Thicken: Slowly stream in warm whole milk while whisking continuously to prevent lumps.
    • Season: Simmer until velvety and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, then stir in salt, black pepper, and a generous pinch of freshly grated nutmeg.
  3. Assemble the Layers:
    • Base: Spread a thin layer of ragù on the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
    • Build: Place a layer of pasta sheets, followed by a thin smear of ragù, a drizzle of besciamella, and a handful of grated Parmigiano.
    • Repeat: Build 5 to 8 thin layers in this exact order.
    • Top: Finish with a final layer of pasta completely covered in besciamella, a touch of ragù, and plenty of Parmigiano.
  4. Bake to Golden Perfection:
    • Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C).
    • Cover the dish with foil and bake for 30 to 40 minutes.
    • Remove the foil and bake for another 10 to 15 minutes until the top is bubbling and deeply golden brown.
    • Crucial Step: Let the lasagna rest for 15 to 20 minutes before slicing so the layers set properly and don't slide apart.
