Tiramisu

Description

Tiramisu homemade for everyone in the family.

An excelent choice for the busy weekend.

Ingredients

  • Prepared Tiramisu sheets
  • Good tommato, fresh from the grocery store
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Put Olive Oil
  2. Put garlic in the hot oil
  3. Throw in the tommato
  4. Lay Tiramisu sheets, cover with tomato sauce. Repeat, repeat, repeat
